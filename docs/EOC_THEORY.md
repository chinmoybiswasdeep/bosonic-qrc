# Edge-of-chaos theory

The closed reference is the fixed-number Bose–Hubbard Hamiltonian
\(H=-J\sum_{\langle i,j\rangle}(a_i^\dagger a_j+h.c.)+\frac U2\sum_i n_i(n_i-1)+\sum_i\epsilon_i n_i\).
Exact continuous, exact declared Floquet, and approximate Strang dynamics are separate model labels.
The symmetric Floquet product is never presented as a Trotter error.

Physical boundaries are located before task evaluation from reflection-resolved adjacent-spacing
ratios with fixed edge trimming and bootstrap intervals, connected/unconnected spectral form
factors, and number/parity OTOCs. Candidate midpoint crossings are descriptive at finite size. This
implements the scientific ordering motivated by Kobayashi and Motome [@kobayashi_motome_2026],
while preserving the distinction from Gaussian dynamical/stability transitions
[@martinez_pena_2021].

The variable-number open reservoir carries a full truncated Fock density matrix. Loss supplies
fading memory; channel contraction and equal-input state distance are open-system diagnostics, not
evidence of many-body chaos. Gaussian universality [@nokkala_2021] motivates the unchanged
Gaussian control, not a claim that it is interacting or chaotic.
