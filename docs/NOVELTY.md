# Novelty and claims audit

The fixed interferometer, Fock inputs, and PNR feature map reproduce standard
static-QRP principles. Deterministic outcome indexing, backend guards, physical
unitary perturbations, packaging, and manifests are engineering work. No
tomography reproduction, quantum advantage, or novel scientific finding is
claimed. Hardware-aware training and reduced-detector tomography remain
hypotheses until multi-seed calibration and primary-literature review are done.
