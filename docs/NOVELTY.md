# Novelty and claims audit

The N+N loop/pulse topology, detector covariance readout, and linear readout are
reproduced principles from García-Beni et al.; no published numerical figure is
yet claimed as reproduced. Backend-invocation guards, explicit beam-splitter
convention tests, unconditional loop persistence, finite-shot backend homodyne,
physicality checks, and manifests are engineering contributions.

No quantum advantage or scientific novelty is claimed. The smoke delayed-memory
run is a gate diagnostic. Loss-aware finite-shot scaling and resource-matched
CV/DV comparisons remain hypotheses requiring a literature review and full
multi-seed experiments.
